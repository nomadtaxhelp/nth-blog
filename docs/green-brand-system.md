# NTH green brand system (approved 10 November 2026)

Use forest #123e32, ink #142a23, paper #ffffff, pale green #edf3ed, sage #d8e9d9, muted text #53645c and rules #d8e1dc. The blue trial is not the approved direction.

Use Instrument Sans for interface/body and Georgia for editorial display headings. Keep functional forms sans-serif. Keep native error, success, provider marks and flags recognizable.

Vary the opening by purpose: full photographic hero on the homepage and paid Paraguay setup page; a white service header with contained photography on /services; typographic headers on guides, comparisons and FAQs; clean functional surfaces for applications, checkout and classroom. Real client imagery belongs with the client's story. Do not add a scenery background to every page.

This release changes presentation, not offer scope, prices, proof, form rules, payment handling, access, tracking or SEO URLs. Preserve those when extending the system. Check mobile at 320/390px and desktop at 1440px. Never change navigation targets to fit a mockup.

The homepage keeps partner-specific booking parameters and the quiz route. Blog related cards use article containers, not nested anchors. Main shared theme loads after legacy styles. Do not restore the old gold/blue palette through stylesheet order.
