You've heard Paraguay gives you 0% tax, a 3-day setup visit, and a proper residency. You've also heard it's too good to be true, or sketchy, or only works if you move there full-time. Most of what you've read is half-right. Here's the actual picture.

## How does Paraguay tax residency actually work?

Paraguay taxes you on Paraguay-source income only. Foreign-source income is 0%. That's the whole mechanism. If you earn from clients abroad, run a US LLC, or hold crypto, none of that is taxable in Paraguay.

This isn't a loophole. It's how Paraguay's territorial tax system is written into law. Over 30,000 people set up residency in Paraguay in 2025. It's OECD-compatible and legitimate.

The setup itself takes 3 days in-country. You visit government offices (immigration, police, identification), submit your documents, and leave with the process underway. Your residency card and cedula (national ID) are processed over the following months.

## What does a complete setup include?

A properly defensible Paraguay tax setup has seven parts:

1. **Residency** (temporary, then permanent after 2 years)
2. **Cedula** (national ID card)
3. **RUC** (tax number, equivalent to an SSN)
4. **Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)** obtained annually
5. **Paraguayan address** (lease contract in your name)
6. **Bank account** (optional but useful)
7. **US LLC** (optional, for income structuring)

The residency card alone is necessary but not sufficient. Without the RUC, TRC, and address, you don't have the full paper trail that protects you if your home country's tax authority asks questions.

## How much does it cost?

## What documents do I need before flying?

Three documents, all apostilled:

1. **Birth certificate** (apostilled)
2. **Criminal record certificate** (apostilled, must be issued within 3 months of your Paraguay arrival date)
3. **Marriage or divorce certificate** (apostilled, only if applicable)

The criminal record certificate timing is the one people trip over. If you get it too early and your trip gets delayed, you'll need a fresh one.

## What happens during the 3 days in Paraguay?

- **Day 1:** English-speaking driver picks you up from the airport and transfers you to your accommodation
- **Day 2:** Driver collects you and accompanies you to government offices (immigration, police station, identification office). The driver speaks basic English, enough to help with forms and logistics
- **Day 3:** Driver transfers you back to the airport, or you stay longer if you prefer

That's it. The rest of the processing happens remotely over the following months.

## What are the ongoing visit requirements?

- **Visit 1:** The initial 3-day setup trip
- **Visit 2:** A short check-in before 365 days from your temporary ID card being issued
- **Visit 3:** Conversion to permanent residency before the 2-year mark
- **After permanent residency:** Once every 3 years

The 12-month consecutive absence rule is now actively enforced via border data (Law 6984/2022). If you miss it, a 2-year extension is available, but you must be physically present in Paraguay to request it.

<Callout type="warning">
**The single biggest mistake people make:** treating the Paraguay residency card as a magic shield. It isn't. If you still spend 5 months in the UK, your home is still available to you there, and your family lives there, HMRC will still claim you. You need both sides: exit the old system cleanly AND build Paraguay ties. Get one without the other and you're exposed.
</Callout>

## How do I stay protected?

You're protected when:

- You've deregistered from your home country tax system
- You spend under 183 days in your home country (4-5 months is pushing it)
- Your economic ties are severed (no businesses controlled from your old country)
- Your family has relocated
- You have evidence of a new life: rental contracts, utility bills, gym memberships elsewhere
- Your Paraguay residency is maintained (visit schedule met)
- Your RUC is maintained with monthly VAT returns or a Paraguay income source
- Your TRC is obtained annually

You're NOT protected if you spend 6+ months in your home country AND your spouse or children live there, or your businesses are controlled from there, or your home is still available to you, or your main economic interests remain there.

## What happens after the setup?

After your 3-day visit, the timeline looks like this:

1. **Residency processing:** Approximately 4 months (subject to government processing times)
2. **RUC and TRC:** Applied for once your cedula is issued
3. **US LLC** (if applicable): Set up in parallel, usually takes 2-3 weeks
4. **Document delivery:** Collected on Visit 2 by default, or shipped via DHL ($70-100 to Europe)

Status updates come through at least every 4 weeks during processing.

## Is this right for you?

Paraguay works best for people who:

- Earn income from foreign sources (clients abroad, online business, investments, crypto)
- Can exit their current tax residency cleanly
- Don't need to spend more than 4-5 months per year in their old country
- Want the cheapest legal 0% tax setup available
- Value simplicity: one trip, one setup, low maintenance

If you're earning locally in your current country, have a family who can't relocate, or can't reduce your days in your home country below the threshold, Paraguay might not be the right fit yet. That's worth knowing before you spend the money.
