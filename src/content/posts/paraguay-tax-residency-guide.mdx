Paraguay can give you a residence base while you keep travelling. To decide whether it works for your taxes, you need to connect three things: your income, where you actually work and live, and the rules of the country you are leaving.

This guide is for non-US digital nomads, remote workers and travelling founders comparing their options. It follows the ordinary temporary-residence route. Nationality and family circumstances can change the route and paperwork.

## When can foreign income fall outside Paraguay's tax base?

Paraguay's personal income tax applies to Paraguayan-source income. The source rules look at the activity, asset or right producing the income. A client paying from abroad is not, by itself, enough to establish foreign source. [Law 6380/2019, Article 48](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9332/ley-n-6380-de-modernizaci-n-y-simplificaci-n-del-sistema-tributario-nacional).

For remote services, where you perform the work matters. DNIT's published consultation 828 distinguishes services performed abroad from services performed in Paraguay for foreign clients. That is a much more useful starting point than the currency on your invoice. [DNIT's service-income analysis](https://www.dnit.gov.py/documents/20123/422349/AFECTACI%C3%93N%2BDEL%2BIRP%2BPOR%2BASESOR%C3%8DA%2BPRESTADO%2BFUERA%2BDEL%2BPARAGUAY.%2BN%C2%B0%2B828%2BCV.pdf/e7fcda99-adbc-e331-d8a1-5d0b6c8950ef?t=1770985417469.pdf).

Before choosing a structure, list your income separately: employment, freelance work, company profits, distributions and investments. Have the relevant adviser classify each stream. Opening a US LLC does not turn every payment into tax-free income.

## How the residency process works

**Prepare before flying.** Confirm your immigration route, entry requirements and document set. Have scans checked before booking appointments. Use the [requirements checklist](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-requirements) to organise originals, authentication and translations.

**Complete the initial appointments.** NTH currently plans around 4–6 days for the first visit. This is a service planning estimate, not the government processing time. [Current NTH service scope](https://nomadtaxhelp.com/services).

**Allow for processing and later documents.** Residency approval, the cédula, taxpayer registration and any fiscal certificate follow their own requirements. Keep separate dates for the appointment trip, approval and document collection. The [timeline guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline) explains what to schedule.

**Plan the permanent stage.** The ordinary temporary permit is valid for up to two years. Migraciones specifies a category-change application window beginning three months before expiry. Permanent residence is a later application, not a document issued on the first short trip. [Temporary residence](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/); [permanent category change](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-permanente-para-el-cambio-de-categoria-de-residente-temporal/).

## Can your home country still tax you?

Yes, depending on its rules and your circumstances. A new permit does not decide whether you remain resident elsewhere, whether local-source income remains taxable, or whether an exit charge applies.

For example, keeping a home, working regularly in a country or maintaining family and economic ties can matter alongside days. There is no universal rule that staying under 183 days makes a traveller tax-free. Use the [European nomad tax guide](/blog/posts/european-nomad-tax-playbook) to identify the questions for your home-country adviser.

Keep records of where you work, your travel, accommodation, income and filings. The records should describe your actual life, rather than a paper address that conflicts with it.
