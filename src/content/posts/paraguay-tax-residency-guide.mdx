If you work for clients overseas, a Paraguayan residence permit answers one question immediately. You have permission to live in Paraguay. It still leaves two questions most people care about. What happens to the money you earn, and what can you show a bank or tax office when it asks where you are tax resident?

You can answer much of the first question before you buy a flight. The second starts with asking the organisation what document it wants and for which year. Here is how those decisions fit together for a digital nomad, remote worker or travelling founder taking the ordinary temporary-residence route.

## Where will you be when you do the work?

Paraguay’s personal income tax, the IRP, applies source rules. Article 48 looks at the activity, asset or right giving rise to the income and includes specific provisions. For an independent consultant, paying attention to where the work was performed can matter more than the country shown on a customer’s invoice. [Law 6380/2019, Article 48](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9332/ley-n-6380-de-modernizacion-y-simplificacion-del-sistema-tributario-nacional).

DNIT considered a consultant who planned to leave Paraguay for five months to advise clients abroad in person. On the facts submitted, the authority treated the portion performed outside Paraguay as outside Paraguayan IRP. It said the portion performed from Paraguay for customers abroad met the territorial test. This gives a practical answer to someone who intends to keep the same clients after moving. Keep a record of which work you do from each country. The ruling concerns those consulting services. Dividends, employment income and company profits each need their own assessment. [DNIT’s consultation 828](https://www.dnit.gov.py/documents/20123/422349/AFECTACI%C3%93N%2BDEL%2BIRP%2BPOR%2BASESOR%C3%8DA%2BPRESTADO%2BFUERA%2BDEL%2BPARAGUAY.%2BN%C2%B0%2B828%2BCV.pdf/e7fcda99-adbc-e331-d8a1-5d0b6c8950ef?t=1770985417469) · [Foreign-income examples](/blog/posts/paraguay-foreign-income-tax)

A useful starting document is a list with one line per income stream. For each, write down who earns it, who pays, what the payment buys and where the activity happens. If the client contracts with your company, distinguish the company’s revenue from the salary or distribution you personally receive. Take that same list to an adviser in the country you are leaving, together with your departure date, homes and working days. You want to know which returns and payments still fall due for the transition year. [Home-country departure guide](/blog/posts/european-nomad-tax-playbook).

## Which Paraguayan document do you need?

| When someone asks for… | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Permission to live in Paraguay | Your residence permit from Migraciones. |
| Paraguayan identification | Your cédula, issued through the Police Identification Department. |
| A Paraguayan taxpayer number | Your RUC registration with DNIT. Check which activities and obligations apply to you. |
| Evidence of tax residence for a particular period | Ask whether they require a *Certificado de Residencia Fiscal* from DNIT, which period it should cover and who will receive it. |

The distinction matters when someone says, “I need proof of residence.” An immigration application, a bank asking for a residential address and an overseas tax authority asking for a fiscal certificate can all use those words. Ask the recipient to name the evidence it accepts before you order anything. [The permit, cédula, RUC and fiscal certificate explained](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-ruc-tax-certificate).

DNIT’s certificate procedure illustrates why the dates matter. For an individual, its resolution requires a migration-movement record for the period being certified. A taxpayer applicant also needs a RUC and up-to-date compliance. The resolution provides a route for non-taxpayer applicants as well. Someone who specifically needs evidence for last year should raise that before assuming a new identity card will settle the request. [DNIT’s procedure, request form and Resolution 65/2020](https://www.dnit.gov.py/web/portal-institucional/certificado-de-residencia-fiscal-para-fines-tributarios).

## How does the first visit fit into the whole application?

**Before you fly**, check your entry conditions and which residence category applies to your passport. Gather the relevant civil records, criminal-record checks and authentication. The issuing country of each document matters; tell the person checking them if you have lived in several places. Take the originals required for your application. [Migraciones’ ordinary temporary-residence checklist](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/) · [Our document guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-requirements)

**During the first visit**, you attend the required appointments and file personally with Migraciones. Our team allows about 4–7 days for that trip. It is worth asking which appointment you will attend each day and who will update you after you leave. No one can guarantee when the authority will issue a decision. [Our current service scope](/services) · [Appointment and processing timeline](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline)

**After filing**, track the immigration decision, cédula application, tax registration and any certificate request individually. Under the ordinary route, temporary residence lasts up to two years. Migraciones publishes a separate category-change application for permanent residence, with a filing window beginning three months before temporary residence expires. Record your actual expiry date when the permit is issued. [Permanent category-change requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-permanente-para-el-cambio-de-categoria-de-residente-temporal/).
