## Why did Ueno Bank go into resolution?

The first thing many customers noticed was that they could no longer use their money. The reason Paraguay's central bank intervened was deeper than a problem with the app. Supervisors reviewed Ueno's loan book and found that some borrowers belonged in higher-risk categories. Once the bank allowed for the losses those loans could generate, its reported Tier 2 solvency ratio fell from **13.85% to 5.56%**. The legal minimum was 12%. Falling below half that minimum triggered the resolution procedure on **2 November 2026**. [BCP board decision, including the supervisors’ calculation](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/356780/Resoluci%C3%B3n%2B1-3.pdf/cb7b69e7-7d1a-8778-c988-0ae8f48e6409?t=1795012809713).

That ratio measures the bank's capital cushion against risk. A figure of 5.56% does **not** mean customers could expect to get 5.56 cents back on each dollar. For a customer, the first question was what they held: money in an ordinary account, a savings deposit certificate, or units in an investment fund. All three could appear through the same platform. They had different routes to recovery.

## Did people get their money back?

**Yes, many did. The regulator has confirmed an advance for eligible depositors, rather than final repayment of every balance.** On 11 November the central bank said it had transferred approximately **₲3.99 trillion** to receiving financial institutions for **1,812,348 depositors** entitled to the advance. Some certificate holders still had to prove ownership before they could use the funds. The figure counts people covered by that transfer; it is not a count of people who withdrew their entire balance. [BCP’s payment report, 11 November](https://www.bcp.gov.py/es/web/institucional/w/aclaraciones-de-la-superintendencia-de-valores-sobre-los-fondos-mutuos-de-ueno-afpisa).

| What you held | What the announcement meant for you |
|---|---|
| An eligible current or savings account | The first payment covered up to **₲121.76 million** per eligible depositor, after offsetting overdue obligations. The central bank provided a lookup to find the receiving institution. [Official payment lookup and instructions](https://www.bcp.gov.py/es/web/institucional/w/comunicado-sobre-el-mecanismo-de-consulta-para-los-pagos-a-los-ahorristas-de-ueno-bank-sa). |
| A *certificado de depósito de ahorro* (CDA) | The receiving institution could be funded before you had access to the money. The regulator required verification of the current certificate holder. It published a separate registration route, including instructions for people without a copy of the certificate. [BCP’s certificate-holder instructions](https://www.bcp.gov.py/web/institucional/w/plataforma-de-validacion-de-registro-de-titulares-de-cda-de-ueno-bank-sa-ya-esta-disponible). |
| Units in a Ueno AFPISA investment fund | The bank deposit guarantee does not cover fund units. The fund manager has to value the units and explain redemptions. On 24 November the securities supervisor explained why redemptions in Ueno Cash Guaraníes and Ueno Cash Dólares had not resumed. Among the difficulties, the manager had lost the banking platform it used to process payments. Its official lookup confirms registration, not the amount an investor can withdraw. [Securities supervisor’s 24 November circular](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/2710676/2026-11-24_CIRCULAR_SVSG-024_UENO_AFPISA.pdf/1a83049b-e803-0aff-4ba3-b9ec67cddb11?t=1795553541885). |

Two numbers explain why one saver could receive everything while another was still waiting. The advance was capped at **40 monthly minimum wages**, then ₲121.76 million. The wider statutory deposit guarantee was **75 monthly minimum wages**, then ₲228.3 million per depositor at the institution, subject to its rules. Suppose you had ₲10 million in an eligible savings account and no overdue debt. The advance could cover your whole balance. If you had ₲200 million on the same terms, a full advance would still leave **₲78.24 million** beyond that first payment. That difference alone tells you nothing about whether the remaining money is lost. [Advance rules under Law 7745](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/2438906/Ley%2B7745_2026-Que%2Bamplia%2Bla%2BLey%2B2334_2023%2Bdel%2BFGD%2B%281%29%2B1.pdf/ca78b392-8fa3-f5b5-4dde-a58e835c7e92?t=1794835942949) · [BCP payment cap](https://www.bcp.gov.py/web/institucional/w/pago-a-ahorristas-prosigue-este-lunes) · [BCP’s guarantee notice](https://www.bcp.gov.py/web/institucional/w/banco-central-inicia-el-procedimiento-de-resolucion-de-ueno-bank/).

## What happened to the president?

On **5 November**, President Santiago Peña submitted a letter of resignation. Congress continued with impeachment. According to the Chamber of Deputies, legislators based the accusation on the financial crisis and alleged conflicts of interest. On **6 November**, the Senate removed Peña from office for poor performance of his duties. **Vice President Pedro Alliana was then sworn in as president.** The resignation letter and the Senate’s decision are two different events. The parliamentary vote established who held office; it did not decide criminal liability. [Peña’s letter to Congress](https://silpy.congreso.gov.py/web/descarga/pruebajuicio-100232?preview=) · [Congress’s account of the vote and oath](https://diputados.gov.py/noticias/noticias/3244).

## What is Paraguay doing about the remaining money?

The first round of payments was only one part of winding down the bank. On **17 November**, the BCP extended the statutory deadline for honouring the deposit guarantee to **2 December**. On **19 November** it described a second piece of work: reconciling the books and grouping loans and deposits that other financial institutions might take over. It reported a *preliminary* estimate of ₲9.2 trillion in assets that could support transfers. That is an estimate of assets, not another payout. The extension does not assign any particular customer a payment date. [Board decision extending the deadline](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/2438906/Resoluci%C3%B3n%2B14%2Bacta%2B54.pdf/58714bae-ea7d-f4f3-b5e1-9e30d50c17bf?t=1795012976509) · [BCP’s 19 November update](https://www.bcp.gov.py/web/institucional/w/bcp-encamina-las-siguientes-etapas-del-proceso-de-resolucion-de-ueno-bank).

There is a separate deadline for affected taxpayers. If unavailable Ueno funds prevented you from meeting a Paraguayan tax obligation due in November, DNIT explains how to request relief for late compliance by **4 December 2026**. You need to identify the obligation and explain how the freeze caused the delay. The tax itself remains due. [DNIT’s notice and filing instructions](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/w/dnit-traslada-los-vencimientos-de-noviembre-y-habilita-medidas-para-contribuyentes-afectados-por-la-resoluci%C3%B3n-de-ueno-bank).

## Does the bank failure affect your Paraguay residency?

Holding a Ueno account was never the thing that gave you a residence permit. The Dirección Nacional de Migraciones grants temporary residence under Law 6984/2022. Its published requirements do not specify an account at Ueno. An existing card does not need replacing simply because the bank went into resolution. If you have appointments booked, a change of president or banking app is not a reason on its own to restart your application. Check the requirements for the stage you are actually applying for. [Migraciones’ temporary residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

There is one place banking evidence can matter. When you move from temporary to permanent residence, Migraciones asks you to document your means of support under the category that fits your work or circumstances. The rules for a remote employee and a self-employed trader differ. If a statement you planned to use is inaccessible, check which documents your category requires before your appointment. That is a practical evidence problem, separate from the validity of your permit. Tax registration and a certificate of fiscal residence are separate steps again. [Permanent residence evidence categories](https://migraciones.gov.py/migraciones-actualiza-el-regimen-de-acreditacion-de-solvencia-economica-para-extranjeros/) · [Guide to the cédula, RUC and fiscal residence certificate](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-ruc-tax-certificate).

## Where can you bank if you choose Paraguay residency?

Felix separates earning, saving and everyday spending in his November webinar. One way to test your own setup is to imagine that your main app stops working tomorrow. Could you still pay for your room, receive a client payment and access your savings? Start with the payments you actually make. A freelancer who bills personally may need a personal account that permits that activity. A founder whose company signs the contracts usually needs an account in the company’s name and a separate way to pay personal expenses. A resident who pays rent in guaraníes has a good reason to consider a local account for that purpose.

- **For local expenses**, Itaú Paraguay publishes the requirements for its digital guaraní account. It asks for a Paraguayan cédula and sets a monthly movement limit of three minimum salaries. Check that limit against a normal month’s rent, bills and transfers before relying on it. [Itaú’s account terms](https://www.itau.com.py/cuentadigital).
- **For company receipts**, Mercury is one option Felix discusses for eligible US companies. It requires real or planned US operations and checks the operating address and owners. Forming an LLC or obtaining a Paraguayan residence card does not secure approval. If the business already banks elsewhere, first ask that provider how a change in the owner’s residence affects the existing account. [Mercury’s eligibility criteria](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770467511060-Eligibility-and-requirements-for-opening-a-Mercury-account).
- **For personal spending and reserves**, check whether the provider supports the country where you actually live, who legally holds the money and what happens if the app becomes unavailable. Two cards issued through the same provider can leave you with the same point of failure. US deposit insurance generally covers up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category. It covers an insured bank failure under its rules; a nonbank app’s own failure is a different risk. [FDIC coverage](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/faq) · [FDIC on third-party apps](https://www.fdic.gov/consumer-resource-center/2024-06/banking-third-party-apps).

For personal accounts, use your **actual residential address** when checking availability. Paraguay does not appear on Wise’s published list of countries where a customer can live and hold a balance. If you look at Bilderlings instead, check the entity in your agreement. Its UK provider is an electronic money institution and describes safeguarding customer funds separately. That is a different arrangement from an insured bank deposit. [Wise’s residence list](https://wise.com/help/articles/2813542/where-do-i-need-to-live-to-hold-money-with-wise) · [Bilder’s regulatory details](https://bilderlings.com/about-us/) · [Its safeguarding policy](https://bilderlings.com/legal-documents/safeguarding).

The account you use to receive an invoice does not decide where you owe tax on the work.
