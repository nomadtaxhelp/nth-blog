Paraguay and Panama both attract people looking for a residence base beyond their home country. The choice becomes clearer when you compare **the route you can actually use**, how much capital it needs and whether you intend to live there or keep travelling.

NTH provides Paraguay services. This guide explains the practical differences using official sources so you can assess Panama fairly, rather than treating it as one expensive visa.

## Start with the immigration route

Paraguay’s ordinary process starts with temporary residence, valid for up to two years, before the applicable later permanent stage. Preparation, the appointment visit and approval are different stages. See our [Paraguay residency guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-tax-residency-guide).

Panama has several categories. One commonly discussed option is Friendly Nations, which is restricted to eligible nationalities. Under Executive Decree 226, applicants demonstrate the relevant economic or professional connection through an employment route, qualifying property, or a qualifying fixed-term bank deposit. The route provides a two-year provisional stage.

| Work and tax questions | Assessed separately from obtaining the permit | Assessed separately from obtaining the permit |

Read [Panama’s Decree 226](https://www.migracion.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/DECRETO_226.pdf) for the actual conditions. Panama’s migration authority also publishes [subsequent decrees](https://www.migracion.gob.pa/decretos-3/), including 2026 changes. The Qualified Investor programme is a different route, so its requirements should not be substituted for Friendly Nations.

## What does the US$200,000 figure mean?

For the Friendly Nations property option, the decree specifies a qualifying property value of at least B/.200,000 and permits local financing. For the deposit option, it specifies at least B/.200,000 in an unencumbered fixed-term deposit with a minimum three-year term. Panama’s balboa is at parity with the US dollar.

Neither figure is simply a government processing fee. Property ownership and locked-up cash have different costs and risks from an agency’s service charge. The employment option has its own supporting documents and work-permit requirements instead of that investment test.

Ask a Panama provider which category they are quoting and whether you qualify before comparing its headline price with Paraguay.

## How much time do you need to spend there?

Separate three questions: time needed for appointments, maintaining immigration status, and establishing tax residence.

For Paraguay, NTH currently plans around 4–6 days for initial appointments. Approval and later documents continue afterwards. Migraciones’ absence rules distinguish temporary and permanent residents. Our [visit timeline](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline) and [maintenance guide](/blog/posts/maintaining-paraguay-residency) explain what to plan.

For Panama, have your provider map the visits and retention obligations for your chosen immigration category. Do not apply a tax-residence day count as if it were the renewal rule for every permit.

Panama’s fiscal-residence framework includes presence exceeding 183 days in the relevant period and a permanent-home route. The [Administrative Tax Tribunal’s residence analysis](https://tat.gob.pa/back/media/uploads/publicaciones/resoluciones/2024/04/04/Exp-019-2023.pdf) shows why owning or renting accommodation is not enough by itself: the wider personal and economic circumstances matter. Avoid a blanket claim that every applicant must spend exactly half the year there, or that a purchased address settles the question.

## Compare your income, not just a territorial-tax label

A territorial system still needs a source analysis. Where you perform services, where assets are located and how company income reaches you can change the answer. An overseas client or bank account is not sufficient evidence by itself.

For Paraguay, our [foreign-income tax guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-foreign-income-tax) explains the service-income distinction using DNIT’s published analysis. Obtain the equivalent Panama assessment for the same income streams. Include your departure arrangements and any continuing obligations in other countries.

## Put both options into the same budget

Compare these separately:

- **Fees:** government applications, professional support, translations and document preparation.
- **Travel:** initial appointments, later visits and accommodation.
- **Capital:** property or deposits required by the chosen route, including how long money is committed.
- **Ongoing work:** residence renewals, accounting, company filings and support.

NTH’s [Paraguay setup cost guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-tax-residency-cost-2026) gives the service scope and separate travel costs. Ask a Panama provider for the same written breakdown, including the later permanent stage where applicable.

## Which option should you investigate first?

**Paraguay merits a closer look** if you want a South American residence base, expect to keep travelling and want to assess the ordinary route without assuming a property purchase is required.

**Panama merits a closer look** if you want to build a home or business there, qualify for an appropriate category and are comfortable with its employment, investment or other route-specific requirements.
