A customer in London pays £12,000 into your limited company after you move to Asunción. Where does the tax fall? Start with the name on the invoice. The money belongs to the company. The company's costs determine its profit, and your own tax calculation depends in part on whether you subsequently draw a salary or a dividend. Your flight date alone answers none of those questions.

## What happens to a UK limited company if its director moves to Paraguay?

HMRC normally treats a company incorporated in the UK as UK resident. A UK-resident company is generally within Corporation Tax on worldwide profits. Keeping the company also means continuing to deal with its accounts and filings. Moving the person who makes decisions may raise additional corporate, payroll or registration questions in the country where they work. Tell your advisers where you will actually negotiate contracts, supervise staff and deliver services. [HMRC’s company residence manual](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/international-manual/intm120030). [UK Corporation Tax guidance](https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax).

There is an important detail for this particular move. HMRC lists **no double taxation agreement between the UK and Paraguay**. If both appear to tax the same income, ask which person or entity each country is taxing and whether domestic relief applies. A tax paid by the company and a tax on its shareholder are two different charges. [HMRC’s Paraguay treaty entry](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt15200).

## A worked departure year for a UK founder

Suppose a founder lived in the UK throughout 2025/26, runs a UK consultancy and flies to Paraguay on 1 October 2026. They have a partner in the UK, retain access to a flat until November and plan a two-week UK client visit in January. Here is what an accountant would need to establish before quoting a tax bill.

| Date | What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 6 April to 30 September 2026 | Count actual UK days, including time away before the move. | The UK assesses the whole tax year first. If the founder spent all 178 days here, another five countable UK days takes the total to 183. |
| October and November | Record the first overseas workday, when the UK flat ceased to be a home, where the partner lived and what happened to the business. | The start of overseas work and loss of the last UK home can occur on different dates. A partner remaining in the UK can also matter under the sufficient ties test. |
| January visit | Record where each night was spent and how many hours were worked each day. | A single visit can add both UK days and UK workdays. HMRC uses a more-than-three-hours threshold in the overseas work test. |
| 5 April 2027 and the following tax year | Check the full-year result, any departure split-year case and its following-year conditions. | A person may be UK resident for the departure year and still qualify for an overseas part. The January visit alone does not settle this. |

These dates illustrate what to count. A founder who spends eight hours each weekday consulting in Asunción has a different fact pattern from a shareholder who receives dividends and does no work. Use the [departure-year split-year guide](/blog/posts/uk-split-year-treatment-when-leaving) and take the actual day and work log to a UK-qualified adviser. [HMRC’s Statutory Residence Test guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rdr3-statutory-residence-test-srt/guidance-note-for-statutory-residence-test-srt-rdr3).

## How will you take money out?

Before changing payroll or declaring a dividend, build a list of payments with four columns: who paid, who received, what it was for and when. For salary or directors’ fees, add the role and location of the duties. For dividends, identify the company, declaration and payment dates and the profits being distributed. If you plan to return, ask about temporary non-residence before extracting accumulated profits. Certain distributions from closely held companies can fall within those rules. [HMRC’s guidance on returning to the UK](https://www.gov.uk/tax-return-uk).

On the Paraguay side, a British customer and a sterling account tell you little about where you performed a service. Separate the company's receipts from your own activities. If you sign and fulfil contracts from Paraguay, obtain local advice on the actual arrangement. Our [Paraguay income-source guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-foreign-income-tax) explains why the location of work matters.

## What to take to each adviser

Give your UK adviser the travel calendar, home and family details, the latest company accounts, payroll records, planned distributions and board decisions. Ask for separate conclusions for your personal return and the company. If the UK company will employ you abroad, raise payroll and social security before the first overseas pay run.

For the Paraguay appointment, bring the same timeline and a short description of who invoices and where the work happens.
