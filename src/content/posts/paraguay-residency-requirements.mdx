The easiest time to fix a missing document is before you fly. Start by choosing the correct immigration route, then have the actual documents checked together rather than ordering a generic “Paraguay pack”.

This checklist covers the ordinary temporary-residence route under Law 6984/2022. Mercosur and other categories have their own requirements. Nationality, previous residence and family circumstances matter.

If you already have a temporary card, use the [permanent-residence conversion guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-permanent-residency) to check the window and proof of income. If you plan a qualifying investment in Paraguay, compare the [Investor Pass and ordinary routes](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-by-investment) before committing capital. For a first ordinary application, start below.

## What can you do before you fly?

You can gather civil records and background checks, confirm authentication requirements and have scans reviewed before travelling. The applicant files the ordinary temporary-residence application **in person in Paraguay**. Keep the entry record from that trip with your originals. Migraciones describes personal attendance in its [official temporary-residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

## Documents to prepare

Migraciones publishes the controlling [temporary-residence checklist](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/). The table below is a preparation aid; use that official list for your individual submission.

| Document group | Where it comes from | Check before travel |
|---|---|---|
| Identity document | Issuing authority | Validity, accepted document type and required copies |
| Birth record | Civil registry | Correct names and authentication |
| Criminal record | Relevant national or federal authority | Previous countries of residence, age rules and current validity |
| Civil status | Civil registry or court | Marriage, divorce or death records if applicable |
| Entry evidence | Your Paraguay entry | Stamp or entry record; visa requirements if applicable |
| Local checks | Relevant Paraguayan authorities | INTERPOL and foreigner police records where required |
| Local declarations | Application process | Address, occupation/activity and required declarations |

Keep a folder for originals and a separate digital copy. In your own tracker, add the issue date, expiry date, authentication status, translation status and the person who has checked each item.

## Which country should issue your police clearance?

Start with the three years before your application. For applicants aged 14 and over, Migraciones normally asks for a current national or federal clearance. If you lived in your country of origin throughout that period, order the clearance there. If you lived in another country for **more than one year during those three years**, the authority asks for the clearance from that country of residence **and a document showing you lived there**. Check [item 6 in the official temporary-residence checklist](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/) for the conditions that apply to your history.

For example, suppose you are British and spent the last 18 months living in Portugal after living in Britain. Start with the Portuguese clearance and ask which document Migraciones will accept as evidence of your residence in Portugal. A UK passport establishes your nationality but cannot show where you lived during those 18 months. If several countries qualify, give the team your dates and ask which certificates the authority requires before paying for apostilles.

Some countries have more than one issuing authority. For Mexican records, Migraciones expressly calls for both a federal and a state certificate in the cases it describes. The same official list also gives different instructions for applicants already resident in Paraguay for over three years and exceptions for certain minors. The overseas clearance is separate from the Paraguayan INTERPOL and police records you obtain for your application. [Steve describes having his documents checked before travelling](/reviews/steve).

For records issued in the UK, the [UK document checklist for Paraguay](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-uk-documents) covers police certificates, legalisation and the order in which to arrange translations.

## Applying with children

A child has their own residence application. Before ordering paperwork, identify who will attend, who has parental responsibility and whether a custody order applies. Migraciones has instructions for applications with both parents, one parent or another responsible adult. A letter authorising a holiday may omit the express consent for the child to live in Paraguay. [Official temporary residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/)

Check the return trip separately. Paraguay publishes different border requirements for visiting children and foreign resident children. For a resident child leaving with one parent, alone or with a third party, check which travel permission or judicial authorisation is required. Review the child’s status on the planned departure date, the terms of any court order and the destination’s entry rules before booking. [Migraciones border guidance for minors](https://migraciones.gov.py/entrada-y-salida-del-pais/requerimientos-migratorios-de-ingreso-y-salida-del-paraguay-de-menores/)

## Apostilles, legalisation and translations

Foreign records must meet the applicable authentication requirements. The Hague apostille route and consular legalisation are different processes. Documents in another language generally need Spanish translation under Migraciones' rules, with an exception for specified Brazilian documents in Portuguese. A foreign translation may itself need authentication. [Official authentication and translation instructions](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

Ask the reviewer to confirm the sequence for each record: obtain the original, authenticate it, translate it and prepare the required copies. Do not assume an ordinary photocopy or a self-translated document will be accepted.

## There is no useful one-size-fits-all expiry rule

Check the validity of the passport, criminal records and any entry documents against the route and planned filing date. Do not apply a blanket “all documents last three months” rule unless the responsible authority actually requires it for that document.

If your trip is delayed, review the dates again. A document accepted for planning purposes may expire before the application is submitted.

Your immigration filing and your tax position have different evidence requirements. Read the [tax-residency overview](/blog/posts/paraguay-tax-residency-guide) if you need to understand what changes for your income after you obtain a permit.

## Before you book flights

1. Confirm the route and entry requirements for your passport.
2. Have the complete scans checked, including spelling and name changes.
3. Confirm which originals and authenticated copies you must carry.
4. Agree which appointments and local documents will be organised in Paraguay.
5. Confirm the itinerary, expected processing stages and any later travel.
6. Ask what happens if a document is rejected or needs replacing.
