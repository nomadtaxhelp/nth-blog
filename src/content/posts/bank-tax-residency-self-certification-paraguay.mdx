If your bank asks you to update your tax details after a move to Paraguay, first look for the name at the top of the request. Is the account yours, jointly held, or in the name of your company? That decides whose tax details the bank is asking for. Then check the date or period the bank wants you to certify. It is easy to send a valid document that answers an entirely different question.

## What the bank is asking

The Common Reporting Standard (CRS) is a framework for collecting and exchanging financial account information. A participating institution uses your declaration to establish how to treat the account under its reporting rules. It may ask for evidence if the answers conflict with details it already holds. The OECD publishes an [illustrative blank individual form](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/crs_individual_self-cert_form.pdf). Complete the version your bank supplies.

| Field | What to enter or check |
|---|---|
| Account holder | For a personal account, use your legal name as it appears on the account and ID. For a joint account, check the request for each holder. |
| Residential and mailing address | Enter where you actually live. Where the form asks separately for a mailing address, enter where you receive correspondence. |
| Jurisdiction of tax residence | Determine your residence under the relevant laws. Disclose all tax residences on a form asking for them, even if you pay little or no tax there. |
| Tax identification number (TIN) | Match each number to the jurisdiction and person it belongs to. For a registered Paraguayan taxpayer, consult the RUC registration details. Follow the bank’s instructions on format. |
| Explanation or declaration | Explain any discrepancy truthfully. Check that the signed form, date and supporting documents refer to the same person and circumstances. |

The bank may also ask about US citizenship or status for FATCA, a separate US reporting regime. Follow that question independently. For an example of how these questions appear together, see [HSBC UK's published personal form, parts 1 to 4](https://www.crs.hsbc.com/-/media/crs/pdfs/uk/crs-i-self-certification-form-uk.pdf).

## Which Paraguay document answers which question?

A **residence permit** records immigration permission. A **cédula** identifies its holder. The **RUC** identifies a taxpayer registered with DNIT. A **Certificado de Residencia Fiscal** can support a claim to Paraguayan fiscal residence for the period DNIT certifies. When someone at a bank asks for “proof of residency”, ask whether they mean a current residential address, immigration status or tax residence. A dated DNIT certificate can answer a fiscal question while leaving an address question open.

Look up the RUC on your own registration record. Do not construct it from the digits on your cédula or copy a company number onto a personal form. If a certificate is requested, check the period before ordering it. DNIT's published procedure requires a movement record for an individual for the period concerned and distinguishes registered taxpayers from non-taxpayer applicants. [DNIT’s fiscal residence certificate procedure](https://www.dnit.gov.py/web/portal-institucional/certificado-de-residencia-fiscal-para-fines-tributarios) and [our comparison of the four Paraguay documents](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-ruc-tax-certificate).

## What if two countries apply, or the bank still has my old address?

Check both jurisdictions' domestic residence rules. Under the OECD's guidance, account holders must disclose all tax residences in the relevant self-certification. Owning a Paraguayan residence card does not by itself extinguish tax residence elsewhere. A treaty analysis used for a particular tax return is also a separate question from the bank's request for all jurisdictions. [OECD guidance on tax residence](https://www.oecd.org/en/networks/global-forum-tax-transparency/resources/aeoi-implementation-portal/tax-residency.html).

Suppose a freelancer moves from Utrecht in June. In October she receives a cédula and her bank sends a form to her old Dutch address. She should first update the bank with the address where she actually lives, then check when her Dutch tax residence ended and whether Paraguayan residence applies. If the relevant statuses overlap, report the overlap according to the form. If the bank requests historical evidence, ask which dates it is reviewing. The October ID issue date alone does not answer any of those timing questions. [Dutch departure rules](/blog/posts/leaving-the-netherlands-tax-residency).

## What if my TIN application is pending?

Check the options on the actual form. For example, HSBC UK's form distinguishes a country that does not issue TINs, a person unable to obtain one who must explain why, and circumstances where local law does not require the number to be provided. These are different situations. If you applied last week, say so and ask whether the bank accepts the application receipt temporarily, whether it has a suitable missing-TIN option, and when you must supply the issued number. Give the bank the application date and reference through its secure channel.

The same principle applies if the status itself is unresolved: tell the bank which fact you are checking and ask about its deadline. Save the request, your response, and any deadline for providing replacement evidence. [HSBC UK customer guidance](https://www.crs.hsbc.com/en/rbwm/uk/frequently-asked-questions).

## What if my business account belongs to a US LLC?

Read the addressee carefully. An entity declaration concerns the company. A separate controlling-person request concerns the individual named on it. If a founder uses both a personal account and a company account, that person may receive both requests. Do not use the company's EIN to answer a question about your personal Paraguayan tax number. If the form asks for the LLC's CRS classification, confirm the entity facts and applicable rules with its adviser before signing.

Finally, read the change-of-circumstances clause on your signed declaration. For example, HSBC UK's published individual form specifies 30 days to notify it of a relevant change and 90 days to provide the updated certification. Another provider can set different deadlines.
