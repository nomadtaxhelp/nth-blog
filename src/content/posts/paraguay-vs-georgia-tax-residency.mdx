For a digital nomad, “Paraguay 0%” and “Georgia 1%” sound like two versions of the same offer. They are not. Paraguay’s territorial personal tax rules address the source of income. Georgia’s small-business status gives qualifying individual entrepreneurs a particular tax treatment for eligible business income.

The right comparison starts with what you earn, where you work and how much time you want to spend in a country. NTH provides Paraguay services; the comparison below separates those facts from our commercial recommendation.

## How Georgia’s small-business regime works

The [Georgian Tax Code](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/1043717), particularly Articles 88–91, provides small-business status for eligible entrepreneur natural persons. The standard rate is **1% on qualifying taxable business income**, generally understood on a turnover basis rather than net profit after ordinary business expenses.

The standard threshold is **GEL500,000**. Where the relevant annual income exceeds it, the 3% rule applies from the month of the excess through the end of the year. The Code also sets conditions for losing the status, including exceeding the applicable threshold in two consecutive calendar years. Some activities and income types are excluded; salary does not simply become 1% business income.

This makes business economics important. As an illustration, 1% of GEL100,000 of qualifying revenue is GEL1,000. If the business has large expenses, that tax is still not calculated as 1% of the smaller net profit. Eligibility comes before the arithmetic.

Use the [Revenue Service’s regime guidance](https://rs.ge/PersonsPreferentialTax?cat=2&tab=1) and have your activity checked before relying on the headline rate.

## What does Paraguay offer instead?

Paraguay’s personal income tax focuses on Paraguayan-source income. DNIT’s consultation 829 distinguishes services performed abroad from services performed in Paraguay. The source analysis follows the activity, not simply the country on the invoice or the location of a bank.

For someone who continues travelling and performs qualifying work outside Paraguay, this can be an attractive framework to assess. Someone moving to Asunción and doing all the same work there needs a different assessment. [Read the examples](/blog/posts/paraguay-foreign-income-tax).

Neither a US LLC nor a foreign payment account turns every kind of income into tax-free income. Review your company and personal obligations together.

## How do residence and travel differ?

Georgia’s ordinary personal tax-residence test includes presence of **183 days or more during a continuous 12-month period ending in the relevant tax year**, under Article 34. Special provisions can apply. Tax residence is separate from visa-free entry and registering as an individual entrepreneur.

Paraguay’s ordinary immigration route begins with temporary residence. The initial appointment trip, subsequent processing, immigration retention and fiscal documentation are separate parts of the plan. A short visit does not answer all of those questions. See the [Paraguay timeline](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline) and [tax-document guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-ruc-tax-certificate).

A useful starting question is whether you want to spend much of the year in Georgia, or establish a Paraguay residence base while continuing to travel. Then check the evidence needed for your actual tax position.

## Georgia’s 2026 work rules need a separate check

The current [Law on Labour Migration](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2806732) regulates work and self-employment by foreigners, with conditions and exceptions. Its scope includes exceptions for certain services provided to non-residents in connection with activities outside Georgia. Consequently, “every remote worker now needs the same work permit” is not an accurate summary.

Equally, business registration alone is not proof that immigration and work requirements have been satisfied. Ask a provider to identify the provision that applies to your activity, client relationships and presence. Confirm the current rule rather than relying on a pre-2026 relocation guide.

## Choose based on how you intend to live and work

**Investigate Georgia** if you want to spend substantial time there and your activity qualifies for the small-business regime. Check turnover, exclusions, reporting, residence and work permission together.

**Investigate Paraguay** if you want a South American residence base and a coordinated setup for an ongoing travelling lifestyle. Assess the income source, departure arrangements, visits and company structure before projecting savings.
