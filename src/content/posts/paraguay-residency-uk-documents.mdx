The most useful first step costs nothing. Write out your country of birth, your current legal name, each country where you lived in the last three years and the dates. A British passport alone will not tell you which office must issue every record in your Paraguay temporary residence application.

## Order the documents from the right office

| Document | Where to start | Check before paying |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate | For a birth registered in England or Wales, use the General Register Office or the local register office. Scotland and Northern Ireland have separate ordering services linked by GOV.UK. | If your birth was registered abroad, identify the issuing authority for that record. Check that the names and dates agree with your passport. |
| Civil status record, if applicable | Get the marriage certificate, divorce judgment or spouse’s death certificate that corresponds to your current status. For a divorce judgment, start with the issuing court. | Where names differ, show the receiving office the proposed records and ask what evidence links them. The marriage certificate and divorce judgment serve different purposes. |
| Overseas police record | For UK residence, start with ACRO’s Police Certificate service for immigration or residency abroad. | Decide which country's records you need from your address history first. If a certificate has a different name or an ambiguous entry, resolve the question before legalisation. |

[GOV.UK’s official certificate ordering guide](https://www.gov.uk/order-copy-birth-death-marriage-certificate). [ACRO Police Certificates](https://www.acro.police.uk/s/acro-services/police-certificates). The receiving authority sets the requirements for the [ordinary temporary residence application](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

### If you spent part of the last three years outside the UK

Consider a British applicant who lived in the UK, then in Spain for 18 months, and who is now back in Britain. Paraguay’s published instructions expressly cover residence of **more than one year in another country within the last three years**. They call for the police record from that last residence, authenticated for use abroad, and evidence that the person lived there. Ordering only a UK police certificate leaves the Spanish period unaddressed.

If several countries feature in the timeline, send the dates to the receiving office and get the required combination confirmed. Ask what evidence they accept for residence in each place. Certificates from another country follow that country's authentication process. [Paraguay’s official temporary residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

## Apostille the correct version, then arrange the translation

For eligible UK records, the UK Legalisation Office checks the signature, seal or stamp and attaches an apostille. Its electronic service excludes General Register Office certificates and ACRO police certificates for England and Wales. For these, budget for sending and receiving physical documents. Some documents require certification by a solicitor or notary before legalisation. Use the checker for your exact document. [UK Legalisation Office apostille rules](https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised).

For an English-language document, Paraguay accepts a translation by a registered Paraguayan public translator. If you use an authorised public translator abroad, Migraciones says the foreign translation itself needs the relevant apostille or legalisation. Agree who will translate the document and its attached authentication before ordering a UK translation. A scan is useful for checking spelling, but keep the originals and plan the certified copies listed for filing. [Paraguay’s official temporary residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

## What waits until you are in Paraguay?

Keep the entry slip or passport entry stamp from border control. The filing list also includes the INTERPOL certificate and Paraguayan National Police record for foreigners for applicants aged 14 and over, subject to the authority's applicable exceptions. The declarations covering your occupation and address are generated when you lodge the application. You attend the filing in person. A legal representative can follow its progress afterwards. [Paraguay’s official temporary residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/).

### A final check before booking flights

Lay the completed documents out in order. Can you trace every change of surname? Are the police records current for the intended filing date? Are they from the correct jurisdiction and national in scope where required? Do you have the attachments to the apostilles and a plan for the Spanish translations, local checks and certified copies? For a child travelling with one parent, check the separate consent or court-order requirements in the official procedure before booking.
