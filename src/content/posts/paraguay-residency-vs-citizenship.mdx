The date that matters if you hope to become Paraguayan is the date Migraciones **granted permanent residence**. The Supreme Court counts from that decision. Time spent preparing paperwork, visiting Paraguay or holding temporary residence does not start the court's three-year clock. A remote worker planning the occasional visit and a family making their home in Asunción might both have valid residence cards, but their evidence for naturalisation will look very different.

## What does each document give you?

| Document | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Temporary residence card | Migraciones has admitted you as a foreign resident for a limited period. |
| Permanent residence card | Migraciones has granted indefinite immigration status. The physical card comes up for renewal every ten years. |
| Paraguayan cédula issued to a foreign resident | You hold a Paraguayan identity document as a resident. Your existing nationality continues to matter for travel. |
| Carta de Naturalización | The judicial decision granting Paraguayan nationality through naturalisation. A Paraguayan passport is a separate travel document you can seek after nationality is granted. |

If your next step is changing immigration category, the [temporary-to-permanent guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-permanent-residency) explains the deadline and proof of income. A [tax residency certificate](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-ruc-tax-certificate) serves a different purpose again.

## Is three years enough?

Article 148 of the Constitution requires adulthood, at least three years of settlement in the country, the exercise in Paraguay of a profession, trade, science, art or industry, and good conduct. The court asks for a certificate from Migraciones and measures the three years from the permanent admission decision. That establishes the earliest point for starting the ordinary application. It does not establish that the remaining conditions have been met. [Constitution, article 148](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9580/constitucion-n). [Supreme Court requirements](https://www.pj.gov.py/contenido/463-carta-de-naturalizacion/463).

For example, if your permanent admission decision is dated 20 March 2026, the three-year anniversary falls on 20 March 2029. Even with that date in hand, you still need to explain where you lived and what you did in Paraguay. A few short visits and an unexpired card do not, by themselves, demonstrate those facts. Likewise, an [Investor Pass](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-by-investment) can change how you obtain permanent residence. It cannot buy the later decision on nationality.

## What evidence does the court ask for?

The court's checklist pairs immigration evidence with records about your actual life. It asks for your permanent residence card and certificate, Paraguayan ID, authenticated copies of **all** passport pages, and a birth record. It also calls for police and judicial records in Paraguay and a properly authenticated good-conduct or criminal record from your country of origin.

The occupation evidence depends on your circumstances. An employee can obtain a work certificate with the employer's IPS and RUC details. A trader is asked for the commercial licence, taxpayer registration and at least two businesses that can give references. The court lists a study certificate for a student and professional qualifications where applicable. If you work online for overseas clients, ask Paraguayan counsel how your activity and residence can be evidenced before assuming international invoices will suffice. The petition must identify both your real address and an address for court proceedings in Asunción. [Supreme Court document checklist](https://www.pj.gov.py/contenido/463-carta-de-naturalizacion/463).

## Can the application be made online?

Since 12 October 2026, lawyers can file **new** cases electronically with the Civil and Commercial Chamber of the Supreme Court. Existing paper cases continue in their original format. Your lawyer uploads the petition and documents, then attends the chamber for digital authentication. You must appear to sign the sworn declaration. Applicants with enabled accounts can follow the case online. This matters when planning travel because submitting an electronic file from abroad will not complete the personal appearance. [Supreme Court's October procedure update](https://www.pj.gov.py/notas/30797-sala-civil-y-comercial-ya-tramita-digitalmente-los-juicios-de-naturalizacion).

## What about dual nationality and long absences?

Ask about your current nationality before applying. Article 149 addresses multiple nationality through reciprocal treaties, and your existing country has its own nationality law. Article 150 also sets out ways a naturalised Paraguayan can lose nationality, including an unjustified absence of more than three years declared by a court. Do not use a rule written for native-born Paraguayans to plan your own case. [Constitution, articles 149 and 150](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9580/constitucion-n).

One last detail explains why English-language guides can sound inconsistent. People often use “citizenship” to mean nationality or eligibility for a passport. Paraguay’s Constitution uses *ciudadanía* more narrowly in article 152. For a naturalised person that constitutional status follows two years after acquiring nationality. That is a different period from the three years of permanent residence before applying for naturalisation. For an assessment of a passport plan, bring your admission decision, travel history and work records to a lawyer practising before the Paraguayan courts. [Constitution, articles 152 to 154](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9580/constitucion-n).
