If you want to know what it costs to get Paraguayan residency yourself, a useful first budget is **US$800–$1,350 before flights and accommodation**. That is our estimate for an ordinary initial application with standard documents. Someone who also wants a company, banking application support and ongoing administration is buying a different amount of work. Our price for that wider service is **$5,950**.

## How much do you pay Migraciones?

For ordinary temporary residence under Law 6984/2022, the government lists **PYG 2,926,925 in cash**. The line includes *residencia precaria*, the provisional status during processing. At present, the same cash amount appears for permanent residence, including a change of category. You apply for that later under the ordinary route. Both figures come from the fee schedule effective 1 July 2026. [Migraciones fee schedule](https://migraciones.gov.py/aranceles-migratorios/) · [Ordinary temporary residence](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/)

| How you pay | Fee for the ordinary temporary application |
|---|---:|
| Cash | PYG 2,926,925 |
| Debit card | PYG 2,991,317 |
| Paraguayan credit card | PYG 3,007,415 |
| International credit card | PYG 3,023,514 |

With an international credit card the published charge is PYG 96,589 above the cash fee. The same table has a different line for temporary residency under the Mercosur agreement, so confirm which application you will make before converting a figure into dollars. Your bank’s exchange rate and any charges depend on how and when you pay. [Payment methods and fees](https://migraciones.gov.py/aranceles-migratorios/).

## What does the DIY budget cover?

Our US$800–$1,350 illustration already includes standard initial government charges and document preparation, including ordinary apostilles and translations. Adding the full Migraciones fee to that estimate a second time would overstate your budget. The estimate does not cover the later permanent stage, a US company or running a business.

Your own records can move the total. For example, if you lived outside the country that issued your passport, you may need a background check from that other country and proof of residence there. Find out which authorities must issue and authenticate your paperwork before pricing a single translation or paying for couriers. [Official document requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/) · [Our preparation checklist](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-requirements)

For a more useful travel figure, add a flight from your actual departure city, a room for the appointment trip and any local help you plan to hire. We allow about 4–7 days for the initial appointments. Check with the person coordinating the application whether you should budget for another visit. [Appointments and later stages](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline).

If you keep the lease and accounting support into year two, our current planning estimate is **US$1,400–$1,800**, including US filings. Using that estimate, the $5,950 package and that bundle of renewal services together come to **US$7,350–$7,750** across the first two years, before travel, separately charged document work or the later permanent application.
