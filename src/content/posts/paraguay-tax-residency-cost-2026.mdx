## What to budget for separately

**Flights and accommodation.** Price these for your departure city and travel dates. Plan around 4-6 days for the initial appointments; this is not a promise that every document will be issued that week. Confirm any further visits before you book.

**Document preparation.** You obtain the original documents and arrange the required legalisation. Translation costs depend on your documents and are confirmed after review. Interpretation at appointments is included.

**Later costs.** Ask for an itemised breakdown of accounting, address services, company filings and the later permanent-residency stage. The initial package fee should not be treated as a lifetime cost.

The included lease is not a hotel booking. Ask us to explain the property arrangement, permitted use and renewal charge before you commit.

## Official immigration fees in 2026

Migraciones' schedule effective **1 July 2026** lists the ordinary temporary-residence application, including precaria, at **PYG 2,926,925 when paid in cash**. The permanent-residence fee, including a category change, is also PYG 2,926,925. These are separate application stages. Card payments have different totals. [Check the official fee schedule](https://migraciones.gov.py/aranceles-migratorios/).

These government charges are not an alternative quote for the full NTH service. Ask which fees your written proposal includes before adding them to the US$5,950 package total, so you do not count them twice. Keep government charges in guaraníes until you obtain the exchange rate for your actual payment date.

For a DIY budget, also price document issuance, authentication, translations, local document costs, travel and any assistance you choose. Do not compare the government's application fee alone with a coordinated service covering several parts of the setup.

## Comparing Paraguay with another setup

Compare the same items on both sides: setup fees, travel, required time in-country, company costs, accounting and renewals. A residency fee alone does not show the total cost of a tax setup.

The [Paraguay versus Dubai comparison](/blog/posts/paraguay-vs-dubai-tax-residency) gives you a first-year and renewal-cost checklist. Our [banking guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-banking) covers account limits and transfer costs to check separately.

Your home-country exit and the treatment of your income also matter. A Paraguay residency card alone does not end another country's right to tax you. Compare your actual expected tax position and costs with a qualified adviser before calculating savings or a payback period.
