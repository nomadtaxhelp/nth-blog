If you are searching for a **Paraguay digital nomad visa**, you probably want a residence base that lets you keep travelling. The process NTH helps clients use is ordinary temporary residence. Understanding that distinction makes it easier to compare Paraguay with countries offering a dedicated one-year remote-work visa.

Paraguay can be relevant whether you work remotely, freelance or run an online business. The immigration route depends on your nationality and circumstances, while the tax assessment depends on your income and how you live.

## What are you applying for?

Under the ordinary route, temporary residence is granted for up to two years. Moving to permanent residence is a later application with its own requirements and timing. Migraciones publishes both the [temporary-residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/) and the [category-change process](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-permanente-para-el-cambio-de-categoria-de-residente-temporal/).

Nationality-based, family and other categories can differ. Before preparing documents, establish which route you qualify for and whether you need a visa to enter Paraguay. Permission to enter as a visitor is not the same as a residence permit.

| Question | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Can I enter Paraguay? | Entry and visa rules for your passport |
| Which residency route fits? | Nationality, family circumstances and residence history |
| What can I do for work? | Your immigration category and employment or business arrangements |
| How is my income taxed? | Income type, source, work location and relevant country rules |
| How do I maintain the setup? | Absence limits, later applications and recurring administration |

## What documents should you prepare?

Expect identity and civil-status documents, police records and the authentication and translations required for your circumstances. The exact set varies, so use our [Paraguay residency requirements checklist](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-requirements) alongside the current official instructions.

The efficient order is to establish the route, obtain the required records, arrange authentication, and have the documents checked before booking the appointment trip. A generic packing list cannot resolve nationality-specific requirements or a document issued in the wrong format.

## How long is the initial visit?

NTH currently plans around **4–6 days on the ground for initial appointments**. That is our service planning estimate. It is not the time the government takes to approve residency or issue every later document.

For your itinerary, separate:

1. Document preparation before travel.
2. Initial appointments and submission in Paraguay.
3. Processing, identity documents and tax-related steps.
4. Any further visits and the later permanent-residence application.

Our [days and processing guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline) explains the difference. Ask for the current itinerary for your own case before paying for flights.

## Can you keep travelling afterwards?

Yes, a residence base can form part of a travelling lifestyle, but maintaining the permit needs planning. Migraciones warns that unjustified absence beyond one year for temporary residents or three years for permanent residents can lead to cancellation. These are immigration retention rules, not a tax-residence formula.

Record the relevant dates and clarify any permission needed before a long absence. Our [maintenance guide](/blog/posts/maintaining-paraguay-residency) covers the tasks to keep visible after the initial trip.

## What does this mean for remote work and tax?

Paraguay’s territorial personal tax system is a separate reason nomads investigate the country. A foreign customer does not automatically make a payment foreign-source, particularly when you perform the work from Paraguay. [Our foreign-income guide](/blog/posts/paraguay-foreign-income-tax) explains that distinction with examples.

If you are an employee, establish which locations your employer permits and how payroll and work authorisation will be handled. If you own a business, review the company and personal position together. Residency does not require replacing a functioning company with another one without a reason.

## How does Paraguay compare with Dubai?

Dubai has several residence routes, including a dedicated virtual-work permit for eligible applicants. Paraguay’s ordinary residence process is a different product with different documents, later stages and travel considerations. Compare the full route and ongoing commitments, not just the visa name.
