For digital nomads, the best tax base is one that lets you keep more of your income while supporting the way you want to live. A low rate is useful only if your income qualifies and you can maintain the setup.

Below is a shortlist of four different options, checked against official sources on 10 November 2026. It is a starting point for choosing what to investigate, not a ranking of every country. Nomad Tax Help provides Paraguay services and has a commercial interest in that option. The fit assessments are our judgement; the linked authorities establish the rules.

## Compare the same four decisions

| Option | Main reason to investigate | Residence commitment to check | Main tax question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paraguay | A South American residence base with source-based income taxation | Immigration stages, travel requirements and fiscal evidence are separate | Where is your income earned, and how is it classified? |
| Dubai / UAE | A substantial home or business base in the UAE | Domestic tests include 183 days, a conditional 90-day route, and a residence/interests route | Are you receiving wages, investment income or business income? |
| Georgia | An operating base with a small-business regime for eligible entrepreneurs | Ordinary individual tax residence generally uses 183 days in a continuous 12-month period ending in the tax year | Does your activity qualify for small-business treatment? |
| Cyprus | A European base with residence and non-domicile provisions | More than 183 days, or at least 60 days with additional conditions | Which income and contribution rules apply to your circumstances? |

The country sections below explain the conditions behind this table. If these distinctions are new, start with our [guide to digital nomad taxes](/blog/posts/digital-nomad-taxes).

## Paraguay: a base for people who want to keep travelling

Paraguay is worth investigating if you want residence in South America while maintaining a travelling lifestyle. Plan immigration, tax evidence, your company and your work locations together.

Migraciones' ordinary temporary permit lasts up to two years and precedes the ordinary permanent route. The appointment visit is one stage; processing and later documents are others. [Official temporary-residence requirements](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-temporal/). Our [Paraguay timeline](/blog/posts/paraguay-residency-days-and-timeline) explains the difference.

For income, DNIT's published consultation 829 distinguishes services performed abroad for foreign clients from work performed in Paraguay. The described overseas services were outside Paraguayan IRP. That supports the attraction for qualifying income; it does not establish a blanket 0% outcome for every online business. [DNIT consultation](https://www.dnit.gov.py/documents/20123/422349/AFECTACI%C3%93N%2BDEL%2BIRP%2BPOR%2BASESOR%C3%8DA%2BPRESTADO%2BFUERA%2BDEL%2BPARAGUAY.%2B%2BN%C2%B0%2B829%2BCV.pdf/9e2c3311-8fd6-0711-db26-fc4aa517784a?t=1770985417003).

**Our fit assessment:** investigate Paraguay if you want a flexible South American base and your income and travel pattern fit its rules. If you intend to work from Paraguay throughout the year, have that precise arrangement assessed rather than relying on the foreign address of your customers.

## Dubai / UAE: useful when you want a real UAE base

UAE domestic tax residence is not always a 183-day test. Alternatives include usual or primary residence with the centre of personal and financial interests there, or 90 days with qualifying nationality/residence permission plus a permanent home or employment/business connection. A treaty certificate may require a separate assessment. [Dubai government's explanation](https://www.investindubai.gov.ae/en/insights-and-resources/news-insights/uae-tax-residency-law).

The FTA separates business activity from wages and qualifying personal investment and real-estate investment income. Natural persons conducting UAE business become subject to corporate tax when annual business turnover exceeds AED1 million. That threshold is turnover, not profit. [FTA natural-person rules](https://www.tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/corporate.tax/corporate.tax.topics/basis.of.taxation.natural.person.aspx).

**Our fit assessment:** the UAE deserves a close look when you want to spend substantial time there or establish a business base there. Get a quote for the route you actually need, including any company, licence, housing and renewal costs. An employee's visa decision and a founder's company decision are different.

## Georgia: check the activity behind the 1% headline

Georgia's ordinary residence rule generally looks for 183 days within a continuous 12-month period ending in the tax year. Its small-business regime can tax eligible income at 1%; exceeding the general GEL500,000 annual threshold brings a 3% rate from the relevant month through year-end. Salary and excluded activities are not simply brought into the regime by registering. Source rules also matter. [Tax Code, Articles 34, 88–90 and 104](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/1043717).

**Our fit assessment:** investigate Georgia if you want to use it as an operating base and your specific work qualifies. Have the activity and contract reviewed before comparing “1%” against another country's result. Immigration and permission to work also need their own current check.

Ask the provider to explain eligibility, taxable receipts, filing frequency and what happens if your activity or revenue changes. This is more useful than asking only what it costs to register.

## Cyprus: 60 days comes with commitments

Cyprus's current Tax Department guidance lists two residence routes: more than 183 days in a tax year, or at least 60 days with additional conditions. The latter requires no stay above 183 days in another country, a permanent owned or rented home in Cyprus, and qualifying business, employment or office-holder activity there. The activity condition must be maintained as required. [Current Tax Department guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/tax-residency-domicility/).

Residence and domicile are distinct concepts in that guidance. Non-domicile treatment is not a general exemption for every salary or business profit. Get a written calculation for your income, company and any applicable contributions.

**Our fit assessment:** Cyprus is worth examining when you want a European home base and can maintain the required presence, home and activity. Budget for that actual life, rather than comparing a residency certificate fee with a different country's complete annual setup.

## Choose based on the life you will actually lead

Start with the commitment you can sustain:

- **You want to keep moving:** investigate how Paraguay's immigration and fiscal requirements fit your travel, then check income source in the places where you work.
- **You want to live and build in the UAE:** examine UAE residence and business routes together.
- **You want an operating base for an eligible solo business:** examine Georgia's activity-specific rules and presence requirements.
- **You want a European base:** examine Cyprus's home, presence and activity commitments alongside your income mix.

These are starting points, not guarantees. Citizenship, family, employer restrictions and the countries where you keep a home can change the shortlist. US citizens and resident aliens generally remain subject to US worldwide-income rules, with relief depending on eligibility. [IRS guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-earned-income-exclusion).

## Compare the first year and the ongoing year

Ask every provider for two written totals. The first should cover setup, immigration documents, professional work and initial travel. The second should cover renewals, required presence or housing, accounting, filings and continuing support. Add expected taxes and contributions separately so a package price is not mistaken for a tax estimate.

Then ask who handles each job, what requires your own attendance and what happens after the initial visit. A cheap registration with substantial follow-up work for you is a different service from coordinated support.

Your [home-country departure plan](/blog/posts/european-nomad-tax-playbook) belongs in the comparison too. A new base should fit alongside that plan from the beginning.
